Orbit Ai privacy policy

Orbit Ai is a private task and calendar app used by its owner, Mohammed Ali Ayad.

What it stores

Your tasks, board settings and account email are stored in the app's database, which only your signed-in account can read.

Google data

When you connect a Google account, Orbit Ai stores a sign-in token for that account so it can create, update and delete the calendar events and Google Tasks that come from your Orbit Ai tasks. It does not read your other emails, events or tasks, and it never sells or shares Google data with anyone. Orbit Ai's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Tap Google, then Disconnect, to delete the stored token. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
